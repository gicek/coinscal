What Coinscal does

A small app that stays out of the way until a price matters.

Price in the menu bar

Keep the price you care about most right in the menu bar.

Desktop widgets

Add widgets to the desktop and Notification Center. They stay current even when the app isn't open.

Pinned to the desktop

Pin your watchlist to the desktop, where it blends right in.

Price alerts

Set price alerts for coins in your watchlist, so you never miss a move.

And there's more, all to keep things simple.

Your data stays on your Mac.

Coinscal fetches prices right on your Mac. It collects nothing, so there's nothing to leak.

  • No account

  • No data collection
